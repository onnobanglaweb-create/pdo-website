Who We Are: Driving Systemic Change Since 1997

Established in 1997 and formally registered with the Department of Social Services in 2000 (Registration No: Nat-295/2000), the Peoples Development Organisation (PDO) is a grassroots, non-profit development organisation dedicated to driving systemic change across marginalized communities in Bangladesh.

Originating from deep rural roots with our headquarters in Singra, Natore, PDO has strategically evolved into a nationwide development force. We act as a critical bridge between vulnerable populations and global opportunities, focusing on scalable, community-driven interventions.

Fully aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and the national development vision of Bangladesh, our multifaceted initiatives encompass quality education, climate resilience, youth empowerment, and digital innovation. By integrating modern technology with on-the-ground realities, PDO empowers communities to break the cycle of poverty.

Leadership Speech

Message from the President

Shaikh Md Rakibul Islam | President, Peoples Development Organisation (PDO)

"Empowering the Grassroots, Engineering the Future"

Welcome to the Peoples Development Organisation (PDO).

For over three decades, my journey as an educator has been driven by a singular, unwavering conviction: true national development cannot be achieved from the top down; it must be nurtured at the grassroots. Having dedicated my life to the expansion of education in rural Bangladesh, I have witnessed firsthand both the immense potential of our marginalized youth and the systemic barriers that hold them back.

Education is the bedrock of societal transformation. When we established Bahadurpur Technical School & College, our vision was not merely to build an institution, but to create a hub for human capital development—a place where underprivileged youth could acquire the technical and vocational skills necessary to break the cycle of intergenerational poverty. However, academic institutions alone cannot solve the multifaceted challenges of rural communities. Issues like climate vulnerability, public health crises, and economic exclusion require a broader, more integrated socio-economic intervention.

This realization is the heartbeat of the Peoples Development Organisation (PDO).

Since its inception, PDO has served as a dynamic platform to scale our community-driven initiatives. We do not view the marginalized as passive recipients of aid, but as resilient partners in development. From our rigorous 'Tiered Scholarship Program' and 'Zero Hunger Campus Model' to our massive climate action campaigns and rural healthcare interventions, every initiative at PDO is designed to foster self-reliance, ensure gender justice, and build climate resilience.

Our strength lies in unity and strategic collaboration. The profound impacts we have achieved over the years—often in close partnership with the SRI Welfare Foundation and our dedicated community volunteers—demonstrate that when grassroots determination is paired with structured, transparent execution, the results are deeply transformative.

To our international donors, development partners, and well-wishers, I extend a heartfelt invitation. Join us in this journey of transformative impact. Together, we can bridge the gap between global resources and grassroots realities, ensuring that no community is left behind.

Shaikh Md Rakibul Islam

President, Peoples Development Organisation (PDO)

Flagship Academic Retention Initiative

The PDO Tiered Scholarship Program

Since 2000, PDO has actively dismantled educational exclusion for marginalized students across Natore. Recognizing that financial hardship triggers peak dropouts during 9th Grade, SSC, and HSC levels, we operate a Needs-Based Tiered Allocation System rather than a generic one-size-fits-all model.

By conducting rigorous background assessments and partnering directly with Bahadurpur Technical School & College as our Central Implementation Hub, we safeguard 1,200+ vulnerable youth annually with pinpoint Value for Money (VfM).

The "Zero Hunger Campus Model" (Mid-Day Meal)

Classroom hunger is one of the starkest causes of rural school absenteeism. Piloted at Bahadurpur Technical School & College, this initiative guarantees daily free, freshly prepared, hot and balanced mid-day lunches for 200 to 250+ students and teachers during tiffin hours.

Over 90%+ daily school attendance achieved within months
Drastically heightened cognitive focus & eliminated midday fatigue
Executed in direct partnership with SRI Welfare Foundation
Ready for INGO Multi-Campus Replication

The "Tree Guardian" Mega Project

Unlike one-off sapling photo-ops that quickly wither, PDO and SRI Welfare Foundation pioneered the Tree Guardianship philosophy. We distributed 10,000+ saplings directly into the care of school children, officially entrusting them as certified "Tree Guardians" who water and protect their plant.

Strict Quarterly M&E Tracking of sapling growth and survival
Annual "Best Tree Guardian Award" honoring dedicated children

Cultivating Lifelong Climate Warriors
Second-Chance Education (SDG 4)

Dropout Recovery & Mentoring

In rural Bangladesh, failing board examinations (SSC/HSC) often brings devastating stigma, acute depression, child labor, or early forced marriage. PDO runs a specialized recovery wing rehabilitating 40 to 50 unsuccessful students annually.

  • Dedicated psycho-social counseling overcoming academic trauma
  • Parental counseling sessions ensuring a supportive domestic environment
  • Technical and vocational training pathways at Bahadurpur Tech Hub
Dignity & Retention (SDG 1 & 10)

The Free School Uniform Initiative

Torn clothes cause children severe embarrassment, leading them to hide at the back of classrooms or drop out altogether. Executed jointly with SRI Welfare Foundation, PDO provided 300+ brand-new high-quality school uniforms to underprivileged children this year.

  • Restoring dignity and parity among classroom peers
  • Alleviating hidden education expenses for extreme-poor parents
  • Proven 100% academic retention among uniform recipient cohort

Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability, and Learning

The PDO MEAL Framework

We ensure every dollar is tracked against quantifiable human progress. Our framework utilizes four live operational loops:

Real-Time Continuous Monitoring

Quarterly plant audits for Tree Guardians and daily biometric/logbook classroom attendance checks for Mid-Day Meal and Scholarships.

Community Accountability (GRM)

Direct feedback loops connecting beneficiary councils, parents, and school cabinets to executive management.

Data-Driven Cross-Verification

Cross-referencing programmatic progress with Upazila Parishad authorities, local school boards, and independent observers.

Social Return on Investment

Value for Money (VfM): Maximum Impact, Zero Waste

Unlike top-heavy bureaucratic entities, PDO avoids costly corporate overhead. We utilize existing grassroots infrastructure and deep community volunteerism.
